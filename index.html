Our Focus What We Stand For

Kazi Glamourzine exists to prove that a student ID can double as a business card.

Every issue, we go behind the products and prices to the person: how they started with next to nothing, how they find their first clients, what breaks along the way, and how they keep showing up between lectures, deadlines, and exams.

Kazi Glamourzine spotlight Central University of Technology students who are building real income and real skill from the beauty and personal care hustles happening in dorm rooms, res corridors, and weekend markets across campus. This isn't extracurricular content. It's proof that entrepreneurship doesn't wait for graduation.
